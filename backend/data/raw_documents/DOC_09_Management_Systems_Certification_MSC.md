# BIS Management Systems Certification (MSC) Scheme

## General Overview
The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) operates the Management Systems Certification (MSC) Scheme, providing third-party certification to organizations that have implemented management systems in accordance with relevant Indian/International standards. It assures customers and stakeholders that the organization follows established management processes.

## Key Management Systems Covered
BIS provides certification for various management systems, including:
### Quality Management System (IS/ISO 9001)
Provides a framework for organizations to ensure they consistently meet customer and applicable statutory and regulatory requirements, while aiming to enhance customer satisfaction through process improvement.

### Environmental Management System (IS/ISO 14001)
Helps organizations manage their environmental responsibilities systematically. It helps improve environmental performance through more efficient use of resources and reduction of waste.

### Occupational Health and Safety Management System (IS/ISO 45001)
Designed to help organizations provide safe and healthy workplaces by preventing work-related injury and ill health, as well as by proactively improving OH&S performance.

### Food Safety Management System (IS/ISO 22000)
Maps out what an organization needs to do to demonstrate its ability to control food safety hazards in order to ensure that food is safe.

## Application and Certification Process
### Application
Organizations seeking MSC must submit a prescribed application form along with a quality manual, relevant procedures, and an application fee. The application must explicitly state the standard for which certification is sought.

### Stage 1 and Stage 2 Audits
BIS conducts the assessment in two stages. 
Stage 1 (Preliminary Audit) assesses the organization's documented management system and readiness for Stage 2.
Stage 2 (Certification Audit) is conducted on-site to evaluate the implementation and effectiveness of the management system.

### Grant of Licence
If the audit is successful and any non-conformities are satisfactorily addressed, a licence to use the BIS Management System Certification Mark is granted for a period of three years.

## Surveillance and Renewal
### Surveillance Audits
To ensure ongoing compliance with the relevant standard, BIS conducts periodic surveillance audits at least once a year.

### Renewal of Licence
Before the three-year licence expires, a reassessment audit is conducted. Based on the satisfactory performance of the management system during the cycle and the reassessment, the licence can be renewed.
