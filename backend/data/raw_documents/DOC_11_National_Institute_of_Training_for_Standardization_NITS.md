# National Institute of Training for Standardization (NITS)

## General Overview
The National Institute of Training for Standardization (NITS) is the training wing of the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS). Established in 1995, NITS imparts training to personnel of BIS, industry, government departments, and consumers on various aspects of standardization, quality control, certification, and management systems.

## Training Objectives
### Capacity Building
NITS aims to build capacity within the Indian industry by equipping professionals with the knowledge of national and international standards, thereby enhancing the overall quality ecosystem in the country.

### Dissemination of Knowledge
NITS serves as a platform for the dissemination of technical knowledge regarding standard formulation, testing protocols, conformity assessment schemes (ISI, CRS, FMCS), and management system standards like ISO 9001, ISO 14001, etc.

## Training Programmes Offered
NITS designs and conducts a wide range of training programmes.

### Industry and Corporate Training
Customized training programmes for industries, focusing on quality management systems, internal auditing, measurement uncertainty, and specific product standard compliance requirements.

### Government and Regulatory Bodies
Specialized programmes designed for government officials and regulatory bodies on the enforcement of standards, understanding the BIS Act 2016, and evaluating market conformity.

### International Training
NITS conducts international training programmes for developing countries under schemes sponsored by the Ministry of External Affairs, Government of India (such as the ITEC programme). This helps in harmonizing standards regionally and globally.

## Educational Outreach
### Standard Clubs in Schools and Colleges
NITS coordinates the establishment of 'Standard Clubs' in educational institutions across India. These clubs aim to sensitize young students about the importance of standards and quality in daily life through engaging activities, seminars, and industrial visits.

### Certification Courses
NITS offers structured certification courses for Quality Management System (QMS) Lead Auditors, equipping professionals with the credentials required to conduct third-party audits.

## Training Facilities
NITS is headquartered in Noida, Uttar Pradesh, and features state-of-the-art training infrastructure, including modern classrooms, computer labs, and a comprehensive library of national and international standards. NITS also conducts regional training through BIS branch offices.
