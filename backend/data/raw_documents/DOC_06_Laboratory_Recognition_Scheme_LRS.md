# BIS Laboratory Recognition Scheme (LRS)
**Issuing Authority**: Bureau of Indian Standards (Central Laboratory & LRS Department)  
**Governing Regulation**: BIS (Conformity Assessment) Regulations, 2018 (Scheme-IV)

---

## Section 1: Objective of the Laboratory Recognition Scheme
### Clause 1.1: Expanding Testing Capacity
To support product certification, market surveillance, and compulsory registration, BIS recognizes external commercial and institutional laboratories that have demonstrated technical competence to test products as per Indian Standards.

### Clause 1.2: Scope of Testing
Recognized laboratories test:
- Pre-licence factory inspection samples.
- Factory surveillance samples.
- Market surveillance samples.
- Consumer complaint samples.

---

## Section 2: Eligibility Requirements for Recognition
### Clause 2.1: Accreditation Mandate (ISO/IEC 17025)
A laboratory seeking recognition under LRS must:
1. Hold valid accreditation from the National Accreditation Board for Testing and Calibration Laboratories (NABL) in accordance with ISO/IEC 17025.
2. Have the specific Indian Standard (IS) methods included in its accredited scope of testing.
3. Possess calibrated testing machinery, traceable reference standards, and trained testing personnel.

### Clause 2.2: Independence and Impartiality
Commercial testing laboratories operated directly by manufacturing companies or having conflicts of interest with applicant industries are ineligible for recognition for third-party regulatory testing.

---

## Section 3: Assessment and Recognition Process
### Clause 3.1: Application and Desk Review
Laboratories apply online through the LRS module on Manakonline. BIS technical assessors conduct a desk review of the quality manual, NABL scope, equipment list, and technician qualifications.

### Clause 3.2: On-Site Witness Assessment
A technical team of BIS assessors visits the laboratory to:
- Witness the actual execution of test parameters on sample products.
- Evaluate environmental controls (temperature, humidity in test areas).
- Verify calibration status of instruments and test result record-keeping.

---

## Section 4: Terms of Recognition & Surveillance
### Clause 4.1: Recognition Period
Recognition is initially granted for a period of three years, aligned with the laboratory’s NABL accreditation cycle.

### Clause 4.2: Proficiency Testing (PT) and Inter-Laboratory Comparisons (ILC)
Recognized labs must actively participate in Proficiency Testing programs conducted by BIS Central Laboratory or NABL-accredited PT providers at least once every two years for each recognized standard.

---

## Section 5: De-recognition, Suspension, and Penalties
### Clause 5.1: Grounds for Suspension
A recognized laboratory's approval may be immediately suspended or revoked if:
- The laboratory issues false, manipulated, or inaccurate test reports.
- Tests are outsourced to third-party labs without prior written BIS authorization.
- NABL suspends or curtails the laboratory’s accreditation scope.
- The lab fails to maintain calibrated test equipment or environmental conditions.
