# Guidelines for Grant of Licence under Product Certification Scheme (Scheme-I) — ISI Mark
**Issuing Authority**: Bureau of Indian Standards (Central Marks Department)  
**Reference**: BIS (Conformity Assessment) Regulations, 2018 / Scheme-I

---

## Section 1: Overview and Statutory Basis
### Clause 1.1: Purpose of the Scheme
The Product Certification Scheme of the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) aims to provide third-party guarantee of quality, safety, and reliability of products to consumers. The presence of the ISI Mark on a product confirms that the product complies with the relevant Indian Standard (IS).

### Clause 1.2: Statutory Mandate
The Scheme operates under the provisions of the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016 and the Bureau of Indian Standards (Conformity Assessment) Regulations, 2018. While certification is voluntary in general, the Government of India has made certification mandatory for certain items concerning health, safety, environment, and mass consumption through Quality Control Orders (QCOs).

---

## Section 2: Eligibility Criteria for Manufacturers
### Clause 2.1: Manufacturing Premises
Licences are granted only to the actual manufacturer of the product who possesses:
- Complete manufacturing infrastructure at the factory premises.
- In-house testing facilities adequate to test the product as per the Scheme of Inspection and Testing (SIT) prescribed by BIS.
- Qualified quality control technical personnel.

### Clause 2.2: Exclusion of Traders and Importers
Traders, retailers, importers, and brand owners who do not manufacture the product themselves are not eligible for the grant of licence under Scheme-I.

---

## Section 3: Application Process & Documentation (Form-I)
### Clause 3.1: Application Submission
1. An applicant must apply online through the Manakonline portal (www.manakonline.in) by submitting Form-I.
2. The application must be accompanied by the prescribed application fee, factory layout plan, list of manufacturing machinery, list of testing equipment with calibration certificates, and manufacturing process flow.

### Clause 3.2: Option for Simplified Procedure
Under the Simplified Procedure (available for all products except those under mandatory certification or sensitive categories like cement, cylinders, infant food):
- The applicant can submit a test report from a BIS-recognized laboratory along with the application.
- The licence can be granted within 30 days after preliminary scrutiny and verification visit.

---

## Section 4: Factory Audit and Verification
### Clause 4.1: Factory Inspection
A BIS Certification Officer visits the manufacturing premises to:
- Verify the manufacturing capability and in-house testing facilities.
- Verify competency of quality control staff.
- Draw samples of raw materials and finished products for independent testing.

### Clause 4.2: Testing of Factory Samples
During the inspection, the officer witnesses the testing of the product in the manufacturer’s laboratory and seals independent samples for dispatch to a BIS or BIS-recognized laboratory.

---

## Section 5: Scheme of Inspection and Testing (SIT)
### Clause 5.1: Compliance with SIT
Every licensee must agree to adhere strictly to the Scheme of Inspection and Testing (SIT) formulated by BIS for that specific product.

### Clause 5.2: Maintenance of Quality Records
The manufacturer must maintain detailed records of tests conducted on raw materials, in-process stages, and final lots. These records must be preserved for a minimum of three years and made available to BIS inspecting officers during surveillance audits.

---

## Section 6: Grant of Licence and Marking Fees
### Clause 6.1: Grant of CM/L
Upon successful verification of the factory audit and satisfactory independent test report, BIS issues a Certification Marks Licence (CM/L) with a unique 7-digit or 10-digit number.

### Clause 6.2: Marking Fee Structure
The licensee must pay the annual minimum marking fee specified for the standard. Marking fees are levied based on the production volume or a fixed minimum rate, whichever is higher, as specified in the BIS gazetted schedule of fees.

---

## Section 7: Validity, Renewal & Surveillance
### Clause 7.1: Licence Validity
A licence is initially granted for a period of not less than one year and up to two years.

### Clause 7.2: Renewal of Licence
The licence can be renewed for up to five years, provided the licensee has maintained a satisfactory performance history, paid all dues, and submitted production statements certified by a Chartered Accountant.

### Clause 7.3: Surveillance Audits
BIS conducts unannounced surveillance visits to the factory and draws samples both from the factory production line and from the open market to ensure ongoing conformity.

---

## Section 8: Penalties for Misuse of the ISI Mark
### Clause 8.1: Unauthorized Use
Use of the ISI Mark without a valid licence, or using the mark on non-conforming products, is a cognizable offence under Section 29 of the BIS Act, 2016.

### Clause 8.2: Legal Penalties
Offenders are subject to imprisonment for a term up to two years or a fine of not less than Rs. 2,00,000 (Rupees two lakhs), which may extend up to ten times the value of goods produced or sold, or both.
