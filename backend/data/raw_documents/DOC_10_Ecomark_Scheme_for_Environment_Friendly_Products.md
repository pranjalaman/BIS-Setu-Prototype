# BIS Ecomark Scheme for Environment-Friendly Products

## General Overview
The Ecomark scheme is instituted by the Government of India and operated by the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS). It aims to label environment-friendly products, providing an incentive for manufacturers and importers to reduce the adverse environmental impact of their products.

## Objective of the Ecomark Scheme
The primary objective of the scheme is to encourage citizens to purchase products which have less harmful environmental impacts. It aims to reward genuine initiatives by companies to reduce adverse environmental impacts and assist consumers in becoming environmentally responsible in their daily lives.

## Scheme Mechanics
### Ecomark Logo
The logo for the Ecomark scheme is an earthen pot (Matka). It symbolizes that the product is environment-friendly, generates no hazardous waste, and consumes less energy in its manufacturing process. 

### Certification Criteria
A product is eligible for the Ecomark only if it fulfills the specific environmental criteria established by the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, in addition to conforming to the relevant Indian Standard quality requirements specified by BIS.

## Product Categories Covered
The Ecomark scheme covers various consumer product categories, including but not limited to:
- Soaps and Detergents
- Paints
- Paper
- Plastics
- Cosmetics
- Textiles
- Batteries
- Food Items
- Packing/Packaging Materials
- Electronic Goods

## Application Process
### Dual Compliance
Manufacturers applying for the Ecomark must apply to BIS. The product must meet both the quality requirements of the standard (ISI mark) and the specific environmental criteria. 

### Assessment and Grant of Licence
BIS assesses the product based on rigorous testing and evaluation of manufacturing processes. If it complies with all criteria, the manufacturer is granted a licence to use the Ecomark logo along with the ISI mark on their product packaging.

## Benefits of Ecomark
For consumers, the Ecomark guarantees that the product is safe and eco-friendly. For manufacturers, it provides a competitive advantage in the market, appealing to environmentally conscious buyers and contributing to sustainable development.
