# Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS) for Electronic & IT Goods (Scheme-II)
**Issuing Authority**: Bureau of Indian Standards (Central Registration Department)  
**Regulatory Framework**: Electronics and Information Technology Goods (Requirement for Compulsory Registration) Order / BIS (Conformity Assessment) Regulations, 2018 (Scheme-II)

---

## Section 1: Introduction to CRS
### Clause 1.1: Objective of the Scheme
The Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS) was introduced under the Department of Electronics and Information Technology (now MeitY) and is operated by BIS under Scheme-II of the BIS (Conformity Assessment) Regulations, 2018. It protects Indian consumers from substandard electronic and IT equipment and prevents e-waste proliferation.

### Clause 1.2: Principle of Self-Declaration of Conformity (SDoC)
Unlike Scheme-I (which requires factory visits prior to grant of licence), CRS is based on the principle of Self-Declaration of Conformity. Manufacturers get their products tested in BIS-recognized labs in India and submit the test report to BIS for grant of registration.

---

## Section 2: Covered Product Categories
### Clause 2.1: Key Product Groups Under Mandatory CRS
Products notified under CRS include:
1. Laptops, Notebooks, and Tablets (IS 13252 Part 1)
2. Mobile Phones and Smartphones (IS 13252 Part 1)
3. Power Adapters for IT and Audio/Video Equipment (IS 13252 Part 1 / IS 616)
4. Television Sets and Monitors (IS 616)
5. Wireless Keyboards, Printers, and Scanners (IS 13252 Part 1)
6. Lithium-ion Cells and Batteries used in Portable Devices (IS 16046 Part 1 & 2)
7. LED Luminaires and Drivers for General Lighting (IS 10322 Part 5 / IS 15885)
8. Smart Watches and Fitness Trackers (IS 13252 Part 1)

### Clause 2.2: Prohibition on Import and Sale Without Registration
No person shall manufacture, store for sale, sell, or distribute goods that do not conform to the specified Indian Standard and do not bear the Standard Mark with unique Registration Number (R-XXXXXXXX).

---

## Section 3: Registration Procedure & Testing Requirements
### Clause 3.1: Step-by-Step Registration Flow
1. **Lab Sample Testing**: The manufacturer sends sample units directly to a BIS-recognized testing laboratory situated in India.
2. **Test Report Issuance**: The lab tests the samples against the applicable Indian Standard and issues a formal test report. The test report is valid for submission to BIS for up to 90 days from the date of issue.
3. **Online Application**: The applicant applies online via the CRS portal (www.crsbis.in), uploads the test report, and pays the application and processing fee.
4. **Grant of Registration**: BIS scrutinizes the application and test reports. If compliant, a Registration Certificate is granted within 15–20 working days.

### Clause 3.2: Requirements for Foreign Manufacturers
Foreign manufacturers must appoint an Authorized Indian Representative (AIR) who is an Indian resident or an registered Indian entity capable of taking legal responsibility for compliance.

---

## Section 4: Standard Mark and Labeling Guidelines for CRS
### Clause 4.1: Standard Mark Design
The standard mark for CRS consists of the BIS "Words & Logo" format:
- "Self Declaration - Conforming to IS XXXXX"
- Followed by the Registration Number: "R-XXXXXXXX"
- Official web portal reference: "www.bis.gov.in"

### Clause 4.2: Placement of Mark
The mark must be visibly and legibly marked on the product, its packaging carton, and warranty/user manual. If the product size does not permit printing on the body, marking on the package and screen (e-labeling) is permitted as per BIS e-labeling guidelines.

---

## Section 5: Guidelines for Series Formulation
### Clause 5.1: Series Grouping Concept
To minimize testing burden and costs, BIS permits grouping of similar models into a single "Series":
- A series comprises models having the same basic design, safety critical components, enclosure, PCB layout, and insulation barrier.
- One representative model (the most complex or worst-case configuration) undergoes full testing.
- Other models in the series may undergo partial or no additional testing, subject to series guidelines.

---

## Section 6: Market Surveillance
### Clause 6.1: Surveillance Mechanism
MeitY and BIS appoint surveillance agencies to pick up random samples of registered electronic goods from retail shelves, e-commerce platforms, or warehouse points.

### Clause 6.2: Cost of Surveillance
The cost of purchasing market samples, shipping to laboratories, and surveillance testing is borne by the registered manufacturer as per statutory provisions.

### Clause 6.3: Non-Conformity & Cancellation
If a surveillance sample fails in laboratory testing, the manufacturer is issued a notice. Failure to rectify or provide justification leads to cancellation of the Registration Number and withdrawal of the product from the Indian market.
