# BIS Care App & Consumer Grievance Redressal Guidelines
**Issuing Authority**: Bureau of Indian Standards (Consumer Affairs Department — CAD)  
**Reference Document**: Citizen Charter & Consumer Protection Manual under BIS Act, 2016

---

## Section 1: Consumer Empowerment Framework
### Clause 1.1: Objective of CAD
The Consumer Affairs Department (CAD) of BIS bridges the gap between standards enforcement and consumer rights. It provides citizen-centric digital tools to check standard conformity before purchase and seek rapid redressal when non-conforming or counterfeit goods are detected.

### Clause 1.2: Digital Tool: BIS Care Mobile App
The BIS Care App is available on Android (Google Play Store) and iOS (Apple App Store) in Hindi, English, and major regional languages. It allows citizens to access verification services without requiring an account or fee.

---

## Section 2: Verification of ISI Marked Products
### Clause 2.1: Verification of CM/L (Certification Marks Licence)
1. Every genuine ISI-marked product displays a 7 or 8-digit licence number (CM/L-XXXXXXXX) under the ISI Mark.
2. In the BIS Care App, consumers select "Verify Licence Details" and enter the CM/L number.
3. The App instantly displays:
   - Name and full address of the manufacturer.
   - Status of the licence (Operative, Expired, Suspended, or Cancelled).
   - Valid Indian Standard (IS) number.
   - Specific varieties, brands, and models covered under the licence.

### Clause 2.2: Red Flag Indicators for Consumers
A product should be reported as suspicious if:
- ISI mark is printed without a CM/L number or with incomplete digits.
- The licence status on the app shows "Suspended" or "Cancelled".
- The brand or model purchased does not match the approved list shown on the BIS Care App.

## Section 3: Verification of Gold Hallmarking (HUID)
### Clause 3.1: Verify HUID Feature
1. In the BIS Care App, consumers select the "Verify HUID" icon.
2. Enter the 6-digit alphanumeric HUID stamped on the gold jewellery piece (e.g., GH39K2).
3. The app fetches data directly from the national hallmarking server:
   - Registration number of the jeweller.
   - Name and location of the Assaying and Hallmarking Centre (AHC).
   - Date of hallmarking.
   - Type of jewellery article (e.g., Ring, Bangle, Necklace).
   - Hallmarked purity grade (e.g., 22K 916, 18K 750).

## Section 4: Lodging Complaints and Grievance Procedure
### Clause 4.1: Categories of Complaints
Consumers can lodge formal complaints via the BIS Care App or the BIS Consumer Portal (www.bis.gov.in) regarding:
1. Substandard quality of ISI marked goods.
2. Misuse or illegal imitation of the ISI Mark.
3. Violation of Hallmarking / fake HUID.
4. Misleading advertisements regarding BIS quality claims.

### Clause 4.2: Turnaround Time for Complaint Resolution
- Acknowledgment: Immediate unique grievance tracking number generated.
- Initial Investigation: BIS Enforcement Wing initiates investigation within 7 working days.
- Testing of complaint samples: Completed within 30 days.
- Final Disposal: Resolution and communication to the complainant within 45 days.

---

## Section 5: Whistleblower & Informer Incentive Scheme
### Clause 5.1: Cash Rewards for Informers
To incentivize public vigilance against counterfeit goods:
- Informers providing actionable intelligence leading to successful search, seizure, and conviction of persons misusing the ISI mark or Hallmark are eligible for cash rewards.
- The reward can be up to Rs. 25,000 to Rs. 50,000 depending on the scale and value of goods seized, as approved by the Competent Authority.

### Clause 5.2: Confidentiality of Informant Identity
The identity of informers is kept strictly confidential and protected from public disclosure under statutory guidelines.
