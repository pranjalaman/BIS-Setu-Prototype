# Foreign Manufacturers Certification Scheme (FMCS)
**Issuing Authority**: Bureau of Indian Standards (Foreign Manufacturers Certification Department — FMCD)  
**Governing Regulation**: BIS (Conformity Assessment) Regulations, 2018 (Scheme-I for Overseas Entities)

---

## Section 1: Scope and Purpose of FMCS
### Clause 1.1: Objective
BIS has been operating the Foreign Manufacturers Certification Scheme (FMCS) since the year 2000. Under this scheme, foreign manufacturers can be granted a licence to use the Standard Mark (ISI Mark) on products manufactured overseas that conform to relevant Indian Standards.

### Clause 1.2: Applicability
FMCS applies to all overseas manufacturing units intending to export goods to India, especially products covered under mandatory Quality Control Orders (QCOs) issued by various Ministries of the Government of India (e.g., steel products, chemicals, cement, toys, footwear).

---

## Section 2: Authorized Indian Representative (AIR)
### Clause 2.1: Requirement for AIR
Every foreign applicant must appoint an Authorized Indian Representative (AIR) before submitting the application.

### Clause 2.2: Eligibility Criteria for AIR
The AIR must:
1. Be a resident of India or a registered legal entity in India.
2. Declare consent to act as the AIR on a prescribed legal agreement with the overseas manufacturer.
3. Agree to be responsible for all compliance, statutory requirements, inquiries, and legal liabilities arising from the BIS Act, 2016 and rules made thereunder.

---

## Section 3: Application Process & Documentation
### Clause 3.1: Submission Requirements
Foreign applicants must submit an offline/online application form along with:
- Proof of factory registration from the overseas government authority.
- Manufacturing process flowchart.
- List of manufacturing machinery and in-house testing equipment with valid calibration certificates.
- Nomination agreement of Authorized Indian Representative (AIR).
- Requisite non-refundable application fee in USD or equivalent INR.

---

## Section 4: Factory Audit and Sample Drawing
### Clause 4.1: Overseas Inspection Visit
1. A team of BIS technical officers visits the foreign factory at the applicant's expense (including travel, visa, insurance, and per diem).
2. The officers inspect manufacturing processes, in-house laboratory equipment, and witness the testing of products against Indian Standards.
3. Samples are sealed by the visiting BIS officers for independent testing in India at BIS-designated laboratories.

---

## Section 5: Financial Obligations & Performance Bank Guarantee (PBG)
### Clause 5.1: Performance Bank Guarantee
Before the grant of licence, the foreign manufacturer must furnish an irrevocable Performance Bank Guarantee (PBG) issued by an authorized bank having a branch in India:
- Value: Minimum USD 10,000 (ten thousand US Dollars) or equivalent in Indian Rupees.
- Purpose: To ensure compliance with the terms and conditions of the licence and payment of marking fees.

### Clause 5.2: Annual Marking Fees
The foreign licensee must pay the annual marking fee in advance or in accordance with the gazetted fee schedule for overseas manufacturers.

---

## Section 6: Licence Maintenance and Renewal
### Clause 6.1: Initial Validity
Licence under FMCS is initially granted for a period of one or two years.

### Clause 6.2: Renewal Criteria
Renewal for up to five years is granted upon:
- Timely payment of renewal fees and marking fees.
- Clean surveillance record without major safety or quality non-conformities.
- Valid PBG maintained throughout the licence period.
